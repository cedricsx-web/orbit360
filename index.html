How to get a clean sphere
  1. Hold the phone upright (portrait).
  2. Turn around the camera lens, not around your body. Pivot your wrist, keep your feet still.
  3. Aim the centre ring at each white dot and hold still. The photo is taken automatically.
  4. Do the horizon row first, then tilt up and down. Finish with straight up and straight down.
  5. Good light helps. Moving people will show up as ghosts.
